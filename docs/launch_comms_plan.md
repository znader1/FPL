# FPLEdge launch comms plan — summer 2026

Positioning (every piece of content repeats this): **FPLEdge doesn't promise the
best team or a top rank. It proposes strategies to win YOUR mini-league, with a
transparent xG fixture model you can sanity-check.** The honesty IS the brand —
every rival tool overclaims accuracy; we show our working.

## The three launch moments

### 1. June 19 — fixture release day (biggest FPL traffic day of the summer)
Everyone publishes a "fixture difficulty ticker" that day using FPL's own FDR.
Ours is different in two demonstrable ways: xG-based ratings carried over from
last season, and a published knowledge file showing exactly what we adjusted
by hand and why.

- **Ship same-day:** ingest real fixtures, publish the ticker publicly
  (consider making /app/fixtures viewable without login for this week — it's
  the top of funnel).
- **Substack post (draft before June 19):** "The 2026-27 fixture ticker FPL's
  FDR won't show you." Side-by-side: our xG difficulty vs FPL's FDR for the
  first 6 GWs, call out 3 concrete disagreements and the reasoning ("FPL rates
  X as a 2, our model says 3.4 because their xG-against collapsed after
  January"). End with: best/worst opening runs, who to build around.
- **Reddit r/FantasyPL + X thread:** the 3 disagreements as a teaser thread,
  link to the full ticker. Fixture-release threads get enormous engagement;
  ride them, don't start them.

### 2. July — pre-season (test + seed mini-leagues)
- **Week 1-2:** soft launch to your own mini-leagues. Free. Ask the
  user-testing questions, fix onboarding friction (entry-ID step is the
  highest drop-off risk).
- **Transfer-window content cadence (weekly):** every big transfer = a
  knowledge-file update = a post. "Player X to Team Y: we nudged their attack
  rating +8%, here's what it does to their opening run." Nobody else publishes
  their adjustments — this is the recurring content engine that demonstrates
  the product.
- **Promoted-teams post:** "How good are the promoted three, really?" —
  explain the cold-start defaults (atk 0.82 / def 1.20), how you calibrated
  each promoted side, invite disagreement (engagement) — readers can see their
  feedback land in the published knowledge file. That loop (reader → knowledge
  file → model → ticker) is the differentiator made visible.

### 3. Early August — GW1 deadline week (decision week = conversion week)
- **"Beat your mates, not the game" launch post:** the mini-league strategy
  walkthrough — chase/defend/differential on a real league, screenshots of the
  AI narrative citing xPts and fixtures.
- **The shareable hook:** every league strategy output is league-specific —
  encourage users to paste the headline into their league group chat. The
  product spreads league-by-league, not user-by-user.
- **If billing exists by then:** free covers ticker + 3 AI calls/GW; season
  pass (~£15-25) unlocks unlimited strategy + chat. If billing slips, launch
  free and convert existing users mid-August — do not delay launch for Stripe.

## Operating rhythm once the season starts
- Per GW: one "fixture swing" note (whose run flips easy→hard in the next 3
  GWs) — 15 minutes of work, sourced from the ticker delta.
- Per knowledge-file edit: a 3-line changelog post. Transparency cadence.
- Monthly: honest model report — where the xG difficulty beat FPL's FDR and
  where it didn't (evaluate vs actual goals). "We publish our misses" is a
  trust moat no competitor will copy.

## Channels, in priority order
1. **Substack** (already have drafts/audience) — long-form, the model receipts.
2. **Reddit r/FantasyPL** — fixture-release + pre-deadline threads; be a
   contributor with data, never a link-dropper; the knowledge-file angle is
   genuinely novel content there.
3. **X/Twitter FPL community** — thread versions of each post, tag into
   fixture-release conversation.
4. **Mini-league group chats** (WhatsApp/Discord) — the real growth channel;
   the league-strategy screenshot is the unit of sharing.

## What NOT to do
- Don't publish projected points leaderboards or rank promises — it invites
  accuracy wars against better-funded models and contradicts the positioning.
- Don't launch paid before the product has produced one "it called it" moment
  users can screenshot.
- Don't skip the disclaimer: "decision support, not guarantees" on the landing
  page and in every post footer.
